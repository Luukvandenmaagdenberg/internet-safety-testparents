🚩 Three warning signs to look for

1. Unexpected messages

Were you expecting the message? Does it make sense for the sender to contact you this way?

2. Suspicious links

Check the actual destination before opening a link. A familiar name in a message doesn't guarantee safety.

3. Urgency and pressure

Be careful when a message demands immediate action, threatens consequences, or asks for sensitive details.

💡 What should you do?

If a message seems suspicious, don't reply or click. Contact the supposed sender using a separate, trusted method to check whether it is genuine.
